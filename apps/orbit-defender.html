Orbit Defender

A top-down space shooter for Android. Asteroids, fighters and the occasional boss fall out of a pastel sky while you hold fire, switch weapons and spend bombs. One canvas, one loop — no engine underneath.

Watch the capture → ← All projects

Overview

Orbit Defender began as a browser prototype and ended up as a signed Android release. The whole game is a single React component driving one Canvas 2D context: a fixed update step, a requestAnimationFrame loop, and a state object that holds the player, the enemies, the bullets, the power-ups and the level counter.

The design is built to be legible at phone size. Every enemy is a silhouette you can read at a glance — fighters dive, asteroids drift, hazards telegraph before they fire. The HUD keeps the score top-left with the weapon and the bomb stock under it, and leaves the right-hand corner to the thumb: FIRE, BOMB, WPN.

What's in the build

  • Three weapons — scatter for coverage, laser for reach, plasma for weight. Switch mid-fight with WPN; the weapon levels up to 10 as pickups land.
  • Bombs — a limited stock that clears the screen, worth saving for the moment the sky fills up.
  • Four power-up types — shield, weapon, extra life and bomb, each with its own pickup sprite.
  • An enemy set with a hierarchy — drifting asteroids, diving fighters, telegraphed hazards, and a boss that announces itself with a warning banner before it arrives.
  • Levels that escalate — spawn rates and enemy mix shift as the counter climbs, so the same sky gets meaner.
  • A high score that persists — written to localStorage, so closing the app does not wipe the best run.
  • Procedural audio — every shot, hit, pickup and boss impact is synthesised with the Web Audio API. No audio files ship with the game.
  • Touch and keyboard — thumb buttons on the phone, arrow keys and space on a desktop, plus a pause menu and a high-contrast toggle for readability.
