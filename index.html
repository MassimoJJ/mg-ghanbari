Apps & Games

Shipped AI-powered products — from a relationship communication assistant to generative games and interactive web apps.

Sentinel Vanguard

3D space combat with tactical boss interception — hangar loadouts, laser / plasma / spread, shields and hull, bombs and overdrive, and bosses that arrive with their own health bar.

Third-person adventure for Android in Unreal Engine 5.8 — floating islands, crystal pickups and a gate that stays shut until the count is met. Motion-matching locomotion, StateTree behaviour.
